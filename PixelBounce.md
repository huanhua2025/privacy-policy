# Pixel Bounce · Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026　|　Policy version: 1.0　|　App version: 1.0

> **In short:** Pixel Bounce is a tool that runs entirely on your device. **We do not collect, upload or sell any personal information about you** — the app contains no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Your photos and your exports stay on your own device.

## 1. Who we are

This policy covers the iOS app **Pixel Bounce** (bundle ID `goos.Pixel-Bounce`) distributed through the App Store and published by an individual developer. The developer and the billing party are the name shown under "Provider" on the App Store product page. Contact details are in section 9.

## 2. What we do not collect

- **No accounts.** No sign-up and no sign-in, so we never need your name, phone number, email address or date of birth.
- **No servers.** The app contains no data-reporting endpoint. Photos, exports, settings and device identifiers are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **No analytics, no advertising.** We integrate no measurement tools, no crash-collection services and no ad networks. We do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and we do not request tracking permission.
- **No access to your whole photo library.** See section 3.
- **No location, contacts, health data or microphone.**

For the same reason, the App Store "App Privacy" nutrition label for this app declares **No Data Collected**.

## 3. Device capabilities we use, and where the line sits

| Capability | What it is for | The boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Live viewfinder, so what you see on screen can become the bead artwork directly. | Viewfinder frames are processed in memory on your device only. **Nothing is saved or uploaded automatically.** A file exists only after you press the shutter and choose to save. |
| Photo library — **add only** | Saving the export you confirmed into your library. | The app requests Add Only permission. It **cannot read, list or scan** the other photos in your library. |
| Picking one photo | Importing a single image you choose, through the system picker (PHPicker). | iOS presents the picker, and **only the one item you tap** is handed to the app. The app gets no library access and sees nothing else. |
| Person segmentation | Detecting the person in the frame and replacing the background. | Performed by Apple's Vision framework **entirely on your device**. No network request is made. |

## 4. Where your data is stored, and how to delete it

The app keeps your work in its own **sandboxed storage** on your device, so that the "Recent" stack and continued editing can work. Each project holds a copy of the source image, the export, a thumbnail and the parameters (grid, colour count, palette, style, aspect ratio, size). None of these files pass through anything we operate.

- **Delete one work:** long-press in the "Recent" stack to select it, then delete. The folder and every file inside it are removed.
- **Delete everything:** uninstall the app. All projects in the sandbox go with it, and **we hold no copy anywhere that could be restored**.
- **After you export:** images you saved to your photo library belong to your library, and you delete them the usual iOS way.

## 5. About iPhone backups (Apple controls this, not us)

If you have enabled device backup in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → iCloud Backup**, iOS may include this app's sandboxed work in the backup that goes to **your own iCloud account**. That is Apple's backup mechanism; we can neither read it nor take part in it. To avoid it, turn iCloud Backup off, or disable backup for this app in your iCloud settings.

## 8. Children and your rights

Pixel Bounce is a general-purpose creative tool. It is not designed for, and not marketed to, children under 13. Because we collect no data at all, no data about children is collected either.

You have the right to access, correct, delete and port your information. **Since all of it lives only on your device, you can exercise those rights through iOS itself:** delete projects inside the app, revoke Camera or Photo Library permission at any time under **Settings → Privacy & Security**, or uninstall the app to clear everything. If you need help, or if you are making a request under the law where you live (for example the EU GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), write to the address below — in practice what we can confirm is that we hold nothing to hand over or to delete.
