# PixelForge Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 15, 2026**
**Version: v1.0**

PixelForge ("the App" or "we") is a professional image-editing application for macOS. We understand how important your personal information is, and we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

**Please read this policy carefully before using the App. By starting to use the App, you are deemed to have accepted the terms of this policy.**

> In short: the App is built around **on-device processing**. It **does not require an account, does not collect personal information, does not use ad tracking, and never uploads your images to any server**. Details follow below.

## 1. Information We Process

### 1. Your Images and Files — Always Stay on Your Device

- Every image, PSD file, RAW photo, and project file you open, import, edit, or save is processed locally on your Mac.
- The App runs inside the macOS App Sandbox. It **can access only the files you explicitly choose or drag in**, and cannot scan anything else on your disk.
- The App **does not request any network entitlement**, so it has no technical ability to upload your images or files to a server.

### 2. AI and Intelligent Retouching Features — All Performed On-Device

- "**Select Subject**," "**Object Selection**," and "**Remove Background**" use the **on-device Vision framework provided by Apple** (foreground-subject recognition); the system model performs the image analysis locally on your Mac.
- "**Content-Aware Fill**" and the Spot Healing brush compute their results using the App's **built-in local image-compositing algorithm** on your machine.
- All image processing for these features takes place locally on your Mac and is **never sent to us or to any third-party server**.

### 3. RAW Development and PSD Parsing

RAW development, layered PSD/PSB parsing, filters, and color grading are all performed locally by the system graphics frameworks and involve no network transfer.

### 4. App Settings — Stored Only on This Machine

To remember your preferences, the App stores the following locally on your Mac:

- Interface language, appearance theme, and keyboard shortcuts;
- Tool parameters such as brush, grid, and ruler settings;
- The recent-projects list (file paths only, stored inside the App's sandbox container);
- In-app purchase entitlement state, which is managed by Apple's system framework (StoreKit).

This information does not identify you personally, never leaves your device, and can be cleared at any time in System Settings or within the App.

### 5. Purchases and Subscriptions — Handled Entirely by Apple

The App offers the "PixelForge Pro" in-app purchase, consisting of:

- **Yearly subscription**: includes a 3-day free trial and renews automatically each year after the trial ends;
- **Lifetime purchase**: a one-time payment for permanent use.

Payment, charges, receipts, refunds, and subscription management are **handled entirely by Apple through the App Store**. We have no access to your bank card, payment account, or billing information. Information collected by Apple is governed by the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Apple retains transaction records in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

**Offer and promo codes:** You may also unlock Pro using an Apple offer code or promo code, entered either through the in-app redemption entry or at "Redeem Gift Card or Code" in your App Store account page. Redemption is processed by Apple; the code itself is not sent to us and we do not record it.

#### Auto-Renewable Subscription Terms

1. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account after the 3-day free trial ends; the subscription is valid for one year.
2. The subscription automatically renews and is charged each year within 24 hours before the expiration date, unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before that date.
3. You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in **System Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions** (or in your App Store account settings).
4. Cancelling during the free trial incurs no charge; during a paid term you can continue using all Pro features until that term expires.
5. Refunds must be requested through Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

### 6. Crash and Diagnostic Data

The App **does not integrate any third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs**. If the App crashes, macOS may generate a crash log. Whether anonymized crash reports are shared with developers is controlled by you in macOS **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements** ("Share with App Developers"). Such data is handled by Apple's system mechanisms and does not include the images you are editing.

## 2. What We Do Not Do

We expressly commit that we do **not**:

- **Collect** any personal information such as your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or photo metadata;
- **Require an account** or ask you to sign in;
- **Upload** any of your images, photos, or project files;
- **Serve advertising**, include an advertising identifier (IDFA), or track you across apps;
- **Integrate** any third-party data-analytics or statistics SDKs;
- **Sell or rent** any information to any third party (there is no such information to sell).

These commitments are also recorded in the App Privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) submitted to Apple: tracking is set to "No" and the collected-data-types list is empty.

## 3. Storage, Retention, and Security

1. **Where stored**: Your files and settings are stored only on your own Mac and in locations you choose yourself (such as an external drive or a cloud-drive folder you configure). They never pass through our servers.
2. **Retention**: You manage local data yourself. You can delete project files and recent items, or uninstall the App, at any time; uninstalling causes the system to remove the data in the App's sandbox container.
3. **Security**: The App is isolated by the macOS App Sandbox and relies on Apple's memory protection, notarization, and code-signing mechanisms. Every file access requires your explicit authorization.

## 4. Your Rights

Under applicable data-protection laws and regulations (including, where relevant, the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, and other local laws), you have the right to access, obtain a copy of, correct, delete, and restrict the processing of your personal information, as well as the right to data portability and to object to or restrict certain processing.

Because the App does not collect personal information and has no account system:

- All of your data lives on your own device, where you can view, modify, or delete it directly;
- If you would like us to explain this policy, you can contact us using the details in Section 6;
- You can also stop using and uninstall the App at any time.

## 5. Children

The App is an image-creation tool and is not specifically directed at children. Children should use it under a guardian's guidance; purchases (including the free trial) are managed through their Apple ID and Apple's Family Sharing / Ask to Buy mechanisms. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
